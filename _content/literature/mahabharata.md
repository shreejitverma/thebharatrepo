The Mahabharata is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India. It is the longest epic poem known and has been described as "the longest poem ever written".

## Synopsis
It narrates the struggle between two groups of cousins in the Kurukshetra War and the fates of the Kaurava and the Pandava princes and their successors. It contains the *Bhagavad Gita*.

## Key Sections
- **Adi Parva:** Origins.
- **Bhishma Parva:** Contains the Gita.
- **Shanti Parva:** Discourse on governance and dharma.

## Timeline
- **c. 8th-9th Century BCE:** Core events (Kurukshetra War) likely took place.
- **c. 400 BCE - 400 CE:** Compilation and expansion of the text attributed to Vyasa.
