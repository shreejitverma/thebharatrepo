Beyond the epics, Sanskrit literature flourished in drama (Nataka), poetry (Kavya), and scientific treatises.

## Key Authors & Works
- **Bhasa (c. 2nd-3rd Century CE):** Known for *Svapnavasavadattam*.
- **Shudraka:** Author of *Mricchakatika* (The Little Clay Cart), a play about a courtesan and a merchant.
- **Vishakhadatta:** Author of *Mudrarakshasa* (political drama about Chanakya).
- **Banabhatta (7th Century):** Court poet of Harsha; wrote *Harshacharita* and *Kadambari*.

## Timeline
- **c. 150 BCE:** Patanjali's Mahabhasya.
- **c. 4th-7th Century CE:** Golden Age of Sanskrit Literature (Gupta Period).
