Sangam literature refers to the ancient Tamil literature composed by poets in assemblies called 'Sangams'. It provides a window into the life, culture, and society of ancient Tamilakam.

## Structure
- **Ettuthogai:** Eight Anthologies.
- **Pattupattu:** Ten Idylls.
- **Tolkappiyam:** The oldest extant Tamil grammar text.

## Themes
- **Akam:** Interior, subjective themes (Love).
- **Puram:** Exterior, objective themes (War, King, Ethics).

## Timeline
- **c. 300 BCE - 300 CE:** The Sangam Age.
- **c. 2nd Century BCE:** Composition of Tolkappiyam (early layers).
