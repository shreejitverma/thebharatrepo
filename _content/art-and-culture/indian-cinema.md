Indian Cinema is one of the oldest and largest film industries in the world, producing thousands of films annually in multiple languages.

## Eras of Cinema
- **Silent Era (1899-1930):** Dadasaheb Phalke's *Raja Harishchandra* (1913) was the first full-length Indian feature film.
- **Golden Age (1940s-1960s):** Classics by Guru Dutt, Raj Kapoor, and Mehboob Khan. *Mother India* (1957) became a global icon.
- **Parallel Cinema:** Spearheaded by **Satyajit Ray** (Bengal), whose *Apu Trilogy* won world acclaim.
- **Modern Era:** The rise of pan-Indian cinema (Bollywood, Tollywood, Kollywood) with massive global reach.

## Timeline
- **1896:** Lumiere Brothers showcase films in Bombay.
- **1913:** Release of *Raja Harishchandra*.
- **1931:** *Alam Ara*, the first Indian sound film.
- **1992:** Satyajit Ray receives the Academy Honorary Award.
- **2023:** *RRR* wins an Oscar for Best Original Song.
