---
title: "The Gupta Empire"
date: "2026-01-01"
tags: ["ancient history", "gupta dynasty"]
---

The Gupta Empire was an ancient Indian empire which existed from the early 4th century CE to late 6th century CE. At its zenith, from approximately 319 to 467 CE, it covered much of the Indian subcontinent. This period is considered as the Golden Age of India by some historians.
