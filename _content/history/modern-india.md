Since 1947, India has evolved into the world's largest democracy and a major global economy.

## Key Achievements
- **Constitution:** Adopted in 1950, establishing a secular, democratic republic.
- **Green Revolution:** Achieved food self-sufficiency in the 1960s-70s.
- **Science & Tech:** Becoming a nuclear power (1974, 1998) and space exploration (ISRO).
- **Economy:** Liberalization reforms in 1991.

## Timeline
- **1950:** Republic Day (Jan 26).
- **1971:** Indo-Pak War; liberation of Bangladesh.
- **1991:** Economic Liberalization.
- **2008:** Chandrayaan-1 moon mission.
- **2023:** Chandrayaan-3 lands on the lunar south pole.
