Founded by **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj** in 1674, the Maratha Empire ended Mughal dominance and became the paramount power in India in the 18th century. They were masters of guerrilla warfare (Ganimi Kava).

## Expansion
Under the **Peshwas** (Prime Ministers), the empire expanded from Attock (modern Pakistan) to Thanjavur.

## Timeline
- **1674:** Coronation of Shivaji Maharaj.
- **1680:** Death of Shivaji.
- **1720-1740:** Peshwa Baji Rao I expands Maratha power.
- **1761:** Third Battle of Panipat; checked Maratha expansion.
- **1818:** Third Anglo-Maratha War; end of the empire.
