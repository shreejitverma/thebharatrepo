British rule in India began with the East India Company's trade, turning into political control after the Battle of Plassey (1757), and formal Crown rule after 1858.

## Impact
- **Economic:** De-industrialization of Indian textiles; introduction of railways and telegraph.
- **Social:** English education (Macaulay), social reforms (Sati abolition).

## Timeline
- **1757:** Battle of Plassey.
- **1857:** The First War of Independence (Sepoy Mutiny).
- **1858:** Government of India Act; transfer of power to the Crown.
- **1885:** Formation of the Indian National Congress.
- **1905:** Partition of Bengal.
