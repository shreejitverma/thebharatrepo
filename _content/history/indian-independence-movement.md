A historic struggle against British colonial rule, characterized by both non-violent resistance (Satyagraha) led by **Mahatma Gandhi** and armed revolution by figures like **Bhagat Singh** and **Subhas Chandra Bose**.

## Key Movements
- **Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)**
- **Civil Disobedience Movement (1930)**
- **Quit India Movement (1942)**

## Timeline
- **1915:** Gandhi returns to India.
- **1919:** Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
- **1930:** Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha).
- **1942:** Launch of Quit India Movement; INA formation.
- **1947:** Independence and Partition (August 15).
