Established in 1336 by Harihara and Bukka, the Vijayanagara Empire ("City of Victory") rose as a bulwark against the Delhi Sultanate in the Deccan. Its capital, Hampi, was one of the wealthiest cities in the world.

## Golden Age
The empire reached its peak under **Krishnadevaraya** (1509–1529), a patron of arts, literature (Telugu and Sanskrit), and a brilliant military strategist.

## Timeline
- **1336:** Foundation of Vijayanagara.
- **1509-1529:** Reign of Krishnadevaraya.
- **1565:** Battle of Talikota; defeat by the Deccan Sultanates and destruction of Hampi.
- **1646:** Final decline of the Aravidu dynasty.
