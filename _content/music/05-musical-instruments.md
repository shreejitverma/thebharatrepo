Indian musical instruments are classified into four categories based on the *Natya Shastra* classification system, known as the *Vadya* system.

## Classification
1.  **Tata Vadya (Chordophones):** Stringed instruments.
    - Examples: Veena, Sitar, Sarod, Tambura, Violin.
2.  **Avanaddha Vadya (Membranophones):** Percussion instruments with a skin.
    - Examples: Tabla, Mridangam, Pakhawaj.
3.  **Sushira Vadya (Aerophones):** Wind instruments.
    - Examples: Flute (Bansuri), Shehnai, Nadaswaram.
4.  **Ghana Vadya (Idiophones):** Solid instruments (metal/clay) that vibrate.
    - Examples: Ghatam, Manjira, Jal Tarang.

## Timeline
- **c. 200 BCE:** Classification in Natya Shastra.
- **c. 13th Century:** Introduction of Persian instruments (Sitar, Tabla evolution) by Amir Khusrau.
